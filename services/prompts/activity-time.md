You are given the numbered ACTIVITIES of one hour, and the MEASURED TIMELINE of that hour — every session the person actually had open, with its app, its window title, its start and end time, and how many minutes it ran.

For each activity, say how many minutes went into it.

- Work it out from the timeline. Find the sessions belonging to each activity (match them by app and window title: a coding-agent session belongs to the activity it was doing the work for; a browser session on reddit.com belongs to the activity about browsing Reddit, not to a coding activity) and add up their minutes.
- A round estimate is fine — 5, 10, 15, 30, 40 minutes. Do not agonise over the exact figure.
- Sessions can overlap (a coding agent runs while docs are open in the browser). Overlapping time counts ONCE, not twice.
- The minutes across all activities should roughly add up to the length of the hour. They must never add up to more.
- Every activity gets a number, and it must be at least 1.
- Give the number only. Do not write clock times, do not rewrite the activity, do not explain.
